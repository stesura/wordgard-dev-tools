# Selection

[`state.selection`](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardState.selection) is a [`GardSelection`](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection). Its [anchor](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.anchor) is the side that doesn't move when you extend it; its [head](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.head) is moved when it is extended (for example by moving the cursor while holding Shift). `from` and `to` are the lower and upper boundaries; it is [empty](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.empty) when anchor and head are at the same position.

- type: [`text`](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.Text) selections hold a single arbitrary range in the document, and represent a cursor when empty; [`node`](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.Node) selections select a single node, for example when clicking on a selectable leaf node. Extensions can define their own.
- It's part of the [state](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardState), which is immutable: a transaction produces a new selection, or [maps](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.map) it through its changes.
- `side` in its JSON: [the side the head is associated with](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.headSide). -1 means it is after the element before its position, 1 before the element after it. It influences where the cursor is drawn (on a line wrapping boundary or in bidirectional text) and where further motion takes it.
- typing: the [active marks](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.Resolved.activeMarks). A cursor's [own](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.Text.marks), when it has them (toggling an inline style sticks until something is inserted); otherwise, the marks inserted content would get from spanning marks on the surrounding nodes.
- The slice: [`selection.toSlice(doc)`](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.toSlice), the part of the document it covers, as clipboard content is. Resolved positions, below: [`state.sel`](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardState.sel), its positions resolved.
