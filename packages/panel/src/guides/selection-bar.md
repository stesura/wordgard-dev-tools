# Selection bar

The selected editor's [selection](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection) at a glance, under every tab. Click Selection for the Selection tab, which explains each field.

- (stored): the cursor's [own marks](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.Text.marks), from toggling an inline style.
- The buttons show the node holding the head in the Structure, log the selection to the console, or copy its JSON.
