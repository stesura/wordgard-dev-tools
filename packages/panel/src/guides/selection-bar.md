# Selection bar

The selected editor's [selection](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection) at a glance, under every tab.

- (stored): the cursor's [own marks](https://wordgard.net/docs/ref/#state.GardSelection.Text.marks), from toggling an inline style: typing gets these, not the marks around it.

## Tips

- Click Selection for the Selection tab: there, show the node holding the selection, log it or copy its JSON.
